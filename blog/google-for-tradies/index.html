How Local Tradies Actually Show Up on Google (Without Wasting a Cent)

Kosta Diamantopoulos · DigiTradie Founder

Every day, people are searching for the exact work you do — blocked drains, leaking taps, bathroom renovations, roof repairs.

If you're not showing up, you're digitally invisible. Simple as that.

Paid Ads Does Not Equal SEO Success

Let's be clear from the start: Google doesn't let you pay to rank higher organically. Your paid ads don't touch your organic rankings. They're completely separate systems, and Google isn't allowed to favour businesses just because they spend money.

So if you want to show up when someone searches "plumber Preston" or "bathroom renovation Melbourne," here's what actually matters.

Google Reviews That Say Something Useful

When a client writes "Thanks for unblocking our drain in Reservoir," that's gold. That's Google being told exactly what you do, and where you do it. That review carries more SEO weight than a thousand generic testimonials.

A Real Business Profile That's Fully Built Out

Photos. Service areas. Hours. Short, clear descriptions. This is what shows up on maps, and around 90% of tradie leads come from maps. If your profile is half-empty, you're already behind.

A Website That Talks Like Your Customers Search

You don't need a hundred-page website. But if all you've got is a one-pager with no mention of the actual services you offer, don't expect Google to send you work.

Start with one service per page. Use the terms people actually search. "Premium plumbing solutions" isn't a search term — nobody types that. You're better off keeping it simple: "blocked drain repair," "gas heater installation."

Don't Run Google Ads Unless It's Urgent Work

Google Ads earns its keep for same-day callouts or emergency jobs. Someone types "emergency plumber near me," you want to be at the top — that's when it's worth paying. Otherwise, build the organic presence that earns trust for free.

And Finally, Do Good Work

None of this helps if the work doesn't hold up. Unhappy customers don't leave reviews, don't refer you, and Google won't reward you. Nobody can teach you your trade — that's on you.

What the right setup can do is make sure that when someone searches for exactly what you do, in the exact suburb you work in, you're the one they find.

That's what wins jobs.
