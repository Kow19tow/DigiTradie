Privacy policy

What DigiTradie collects, why, and who sees it. Short and plain.

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

Who we are

DigiTradie is a marketing service for small and medium businesses in Australia, run by Kosta Diamantopoulos. You can contact us at digitradieau@gmail.com or on 0409 025 904.

What we collect

  • Details you give us. When you complete the fit-check form: your name, business name, email, phone number, what you need and your approximate monthly ad spend.
  • Where you came from. The page you landed on, the site that referred you, and campaign details such as UTM tags or an ad click ID, so we can see which ads or searches bring enquiries.
  • Site usage. Anonymous information about how the site is used, such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location, collected through Google Analytics and the Google Ads tag.

How we use it

To reply to your enquiry and run your fit-check call, to understand which marketing brings enquiries, and to improve this website. We do not sell your details.

Who else handles it

  • Formspree receives form submissions and forwards them to us by email.
  • Google (Analytics and Ads) receives site usage information through cookies and tags, under its own privacy policy.
  • Vercel hosts this website.

Cookies

Google Analytics and the Google Ads tag set cookies to measure visits and ad performance. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing digitradieau@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
