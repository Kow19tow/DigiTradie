DIY Guide

How to Market a Hospitality Business

Your organic content is your free testing ground. Post often, see what people love, then put paid money behind the winners only.

Why it works

People choose where to eat and drink with their eyes. A great-looking dish, a packed room or a bartender with personality sells harder than any offer. Social platforms reward that kind of content, so you can learn a lot for $0.

How to do it

  • Post consistently. Food, drinks, the room, the team, the regulars.
  • Be distinct. If your post could belong to any venue in town, it won't stand out.
  • Watch what gets saves, shares and comments, not just likes.
  • Boost or run ads on the posts that already performed. They've earned it.
  • Keep testing. Whatever wins this month becomes next month's ad.

The trap

Paying to promote content nobody engaged with organically. If it didn't land for free, money rarely fixes it.

Also keep your Google Business Profile updated with hours, menu and photos. Plenty of bookings start with a "near me" search.

What To Watch Out For

  • Vanity metrics. Likes don't fill tables. Track bookings, enquiries and walk-ins.
  • Targeting too wide. Promote to people close enough to actually visit.
  • Panic boosting. Throwing money at a quiet week rarely works. Plan promotions ahead of slow periods.

Your Industry Learnings

  • Organic is your test lab. Paid is the amplifier.
  • Distinct beats polished. Personality sells harder than perfect photos.
  • This month's winning post is next month's ad.

Do this week

  • Post 3 times, each a different angle (food, people, atmosphere)
  • Pick the best performer and boost it with a small budget
  • Note what worked so you can do more of it

FAQs

Do I need to pay for ads to grow my restaurant or cafe?

Not at first. Organic content shows you what people love for free. Only pay to boost posts that have already proven themselves.

What content works best for hospitality?

Content that could only come from your venue: signature dishes, your team, the room when it's full. Generic food shots blend in.

Should I focus on Google or social media?

Both, for different jobs. Social builds the craving, and your Google Business Profile catches people searching nearby.
