DIY guide: Law Firms

Law Firm Marketing That Brings In Clients Who Can Pay

Legal clicks are some of the most expensive in Australia, so every enquiry has to count. Target the right cases, qualify hard, and respond fast.

Kosta Diamantopoulos · DigiTradie Founder2 min read · Updated 30 Oct 2026

On this page

  1. Be specific, not general
  2. Trust is the product
  3. Speed wins the matter
  4. What To Watch Out For
  5. Your Industry Learnings
  6. Do this week
  7. FAQs

Be specific, not general

"Lawyer near me" is expensive and vague. Target the work you actually want, like family law, conveyancing or wills, in the areas you actually serve. Specific searches cost less and bring people who need exactly what you do.

Trust is the product

People contact a lawyer during stressful moments. Clear pages, plain-English explanations, real credentials and reviews do more than clever ads. Make it obvious what happens after they get in touch.

Speed wins the matter

Most people contact more than one firm. The first to respond properly often gets the job, so have a process for same-day callbacks.

What To Watch Out For

  • Advertising rules. Legal advertising is regulated, and some areas, like personal injury, have strict limits in certain states. Check your state's rules before running ads.
  • Broad keywords. Paying for searches from students or people wanting free advice drains budget fast.
  • Slow intake. Expensive leads left for a day are wasted money.

Your Industry Learnings

  • High cost per click means qualification matters more than volume.
  • Specific practice areas beat general "lawyer" terms.
  • Trust and response time close the matter.

Do this week

  • List the 2-3 practice areas you most want more work in
  • Check how fast your firm currently returns enquiries
  • Add a clear "what happens next" section to your contact page

FAQs

Are Google Ads worth it for lawyers?

They can be, if you target specific practice areas and qualify enquiries well. Broad targeting gets expensive quickly.

Why are legal ads so expensive?

A single client can be worth thousands, so firms compete hard for the same searches.

What makes someone choose one firm over another?

Trust, clarity and speed. The firm that explains things simply and calls back first usually wins.
